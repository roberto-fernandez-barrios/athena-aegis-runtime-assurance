checked on publisher/proceedings pages where available; arXiv-only works are
labelled as such. This is a bounded comparison, not a claim that no related work

## Frozen novelty statement

Paper 2.5 studies whether observable execution context can serve as an explicit,
testable validity precondition for calibrated hybrid-service assurance under
longitudinal QPU non-stationarity, and whether pre-decision OOS abstention plus
enforced recovery reduces unsafe continuation while preserving acceptable
availability, latency, and fallback correctness.

The novelty excludes conformal calibration itself, false-alarm calibration,
contracts, hashes, generic provenance, local signing, blind-region arguments,
and local allow/hold/block vocabulary. Physical-QPU, longitudinal, and recovery
claims remain killed until their preregistered empirical conditions are met.
